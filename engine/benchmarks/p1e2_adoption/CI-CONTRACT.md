# P1e2 isolated adoption validation

This controller is additive to d9e76602d5edef7cd16891e15ebc6ab7065ff987. The candidate source is test-only atop a10adfd9aeaebe9624bd8e4bd804789ddecf6167; all P1e runtime bytes remain pinned. Original a4a881420ca35335c6e0ac4c73fd9e686dd617c0 receives the same public test, expanded harness and exporter, with exact manifest registration append only. Production and KEEP-R1 do not change.

Two independent jobs run on the controller branch push. The accuracy job runs original/OFF/ON under generic x86-64, canonical core6, one CPU and no observer; ON adds only experiment-lazy-ko-damage. Each fresh expanded suite has 300 s / 6 GiB bounds. Its exact fixed manifest identifies 104 cases, 101 distinct input/decision pairs and 9 smoke cases. Smoke cannot satisfy the expanded gate. The inherited 55 named tests plus 6 expanded named tests give 61 fresh test executions. Full State and full Suspension support, all input/decision/resumption metadata, mechanic activation witnesses and seeded samples must match exactly. Per-key probability error is at most 1e-12, each raw mass is within 1e-12 of one, and each case L1/2 is at most 1e-9.

The separate exact-reference job runs six exporter selftests per arm, then original opening-0429 for at most 1200 s and ON for at most 300 s, each with one CPU and 6 GiB. Both selection descriptions must equal the frozen original plan bytes. ON is attempted after an incomplete original. The exporter writes a complete non-HP State/Suspension dictionary and the correlated joint tuple of all 12 party HP values. The controller streams all support, checks exact dictionary/key equality, per-key raw error at most 1e-12, raw mass within 1e-9 and normalized total variation at most 1e-9. Export limits are 10 million unique rows and 512 MiB including the final manifest. Child exit 0, complete final manifest and identical stdout summary are required; timeout, resource cutoff, nonzero exit or partial files are inconclusive. An invalid claimed complete export is a failed gate. Incomplete reference forbids adoption even if the workflow exits successfully.

Separate target directories and fresh tests are required. Only dependency compilation cache may be restored. Actual compiler fingerprints, binary hashes, immutable input/source hashes, raw stdout/stderr, full broad records, joint payloads, process CPU/wall/RSS accounting and failed partial exports are retained. The accuracy job has 30 minutes and exact-reference job 35 minutes. These are accuracy/feasibility checks; neither job authorizes adoption, computes a paired speed ratio, reruns the full500 benchmark, or repairs its incomplete metrics. A later root review must consider both artifacts and all limitations.

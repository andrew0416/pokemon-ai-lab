Combined totals across four VMs are ancillary, not a single-machine runtime. Five heaviest OFF cases and their shares reveal concentration. No significance or adoption claim is implied.

All raw and partial evidence is retained. Case-local timing files avoid repeatedly rewriting a7000-row ledger.

Existing sources, controllers, workflow files, corpora, exporters and benchmark source are unchanged. This is scoped validation of a new runtime candidate, not a repair of the original-a4 missing oracle, a global mechanics proof or automatic adoption.
